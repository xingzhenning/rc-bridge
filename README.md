# 中文

## 📌 项目简介

RC Bridge 是一个 AI 辅助的科研匹配与职业发展平台，旨在减少寻找科研机会的学生与寻找合适学生的导师之间的信息不对称。

平台不仅关注学生与导师之间的一次性匹配，也尝试通过 AI 对学生的简历、科研能力和发展方向进行结构化分析，为长期科研能力发展和职业规划提供支持。

## 🎯 项目解决的问题

学生在寻找科研机会时，通常面临以下问题：

- 难以判断自己的背景是否符合某个科研机会的要求
- 很难系统评估自己的科研能力
- 导师也难以快速找到与课题要求匹配的学生
- 传统招聘与科研信息平台缺乏针对科研发展的长期能力评估

RC Bridge 希望利用 AI 辅助完成科研供需之间的信息结构化与匹配。

## 🚀 核心功能

### 1. AI 综合简历评估

根据学生的教育背景、成绩、科研、实习、竞赛和技能等信息，对简历进行结构化分析。

### 2. AI 科研能力评估

根据不同研究方向所需要的能力，对学生当前的科研基础进行分析，并识别需要进一步提升的部分。

### 3. 科研匹配

帮助学生和导师基于研究方向、技能需求和个人背景进行更加有效的匹配。

# English

## 📌 Overview

RC Bridge is an AI-assisted research matching and career development platform designed to reduce information asymmetry between students seeking research opportunities and supervisors looking for suitable candidates.

Beyond one-time matching, the platform explores how AI can help structure students' resumes, research capabilities, and career goals to support longer-term research development and career planning.

## 🎯 Problem

Students seeking research opportunities often face several challenges:

- Difficulty evaluating whether their background matches a research opportunity
- Lack of systematic assessment of research capability
- Difficulty for supervisors to efficiently identify suitable students
- Limited long-term research-development support from traditional recruitment platforms

RC Bridge uses AI to structure and improve the matching process between research opportunities and student profiles.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. AI Resume Evaluation

Structures and evaluates information including academic background, GPA, research experience, internships, competitions, and technical skills.

### 2. Research Capability Assessment

Evaluates students' current research capabilities against the requirements of different research directions and identifies potential areas for improvement.

### 3. Research Matching

Supports matching between students and supervisors based on research interests, skill requirements, and individual backgrounds.
